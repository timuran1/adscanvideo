How to analyze a video podcast: transcript, speakers and chapters

A useful podcast report helps you return to the recording. Before a transcript becomes a quote, chapter list or edit brief, check who is speaking, what they actually said and which version of the episode the timestamp belongs to.

Published September 20, 2026 · Updated October 18, 2026 · 8 min read

Start here: upload a recording you own or may analyze, choose Podcast notes, and use the result as a review map. Confirm names, quotations and clip boundaries against the source. Download the free podcast review worksheet (CSV).

Keep a source record before generating notes

Record the episode filename, edit version, duration and the part you uploaded. Keep the original master available to the editor. If a producer sends a shorter review copy, write down where it begins in the master and whether any material was removed inside that copy.

Check that the voices can be heard without competing music and that important slides or demonstrations are visible. Remove unrelated private material before uploading. A recording with overlapping speech may need more manual correction. Decide whether the next deliverable is an internal brief, a public quote, chapters or a timed transcript; each needs a different final check.

Use Podcast notes for a first pass

  1. Upload a video file. The current analyzer accepts file uploads, not pasted platform URLs. One daily on-screen analysis is free without signup, up to 10 minutes and 200 MB. Monthly Premium supports up to 45 minutes and 200 MB per upload. Use a permitted review excerpt when the episode exceeds your limit.
  2. Choose Podcast notes. Review the generated dialogue, speaker cues and takeaways that are actually present. Do not assume every episode produces a complete verbatim transcript or the same fields.
  3. Return to candidate passages. Treat report times as approximate locations. Listen around the passage so that the question, answer, qualification and interruption are still understandable.
  4. Write the editorial deliverable. Keep the generated wording separate from your corrections and your own clip or chapter suggestions. Only publish the parts you have checked.

The current workflow processes video and available audio. Brief visual details, names and speech can still be missed or misinterpreted. For audio-focused checking, see extracting dialogue from video; for a review plan, see how to verify an AI video analysis.

If the evidence is insufficient, keep a neutral label and mark attribution Unconfirmed. A correct name in episode notes also does not prove that person spoke every line.

Apple's transcript guidance recommends including host and guest names in show and episode descriptions to help name spelling. It also explains how supplied VTT files can identify speakers. Those are publishing-platform features, not automatic identity verification in AdScanVideo.

Review a quote separately from a takeaway

A takeaway may summarize several sentences; a direct quote should reflect the words you verified in the recording. Mark which kind of text you intend to publish. When a word is uncertain, replay it or ask the producer instead of quietly replacing it with a plausible term.

  • Who: distinguish the report label from the confirmed speaker name.
  • Words: check names, numbers, specialist terms and any negation.
  • Context: retain the condition, warning or question that changes the meaning.
  • Evidence: save the source version and a useful replay range.
  • Decision: mark the passage Confirmed, Needs correction or Not reviewed.

These are manual review states, not product-generated quality scores. The semiconductor sample's technical statements are reported speech and are not independently endorsed by AdScanVideo. A clear transcript is not a fact-check of the speaker's claims.

Map excerpt timestamps back to the master

Keep two clocks when analyzing a review excerpt: the time in the uploaded file and the corresponding location in the full episode. Here is a constructed timing example, not generated output: an uncut excerpt starts at master 12:30; a report passage appears around excerpt 00:20. The first place to inspect in the master is around 12:50.

That addition is only a starting point when the excerpt runs continuously at the same speed. If the review copy removes pauses, contains inserted audio, changes speed or combines several ranges, a single offset is insufficient. Record a mapping for each retained segment and confirm it by playback. Recheck the published edit after revisions; an older transcript's clock may no longer match.

Apple also documents a limitation when dynamically inserted audio changes after transcription: changed segments may not appear in its transcript view. This is another reason to track which media version a transcript describes. Do not assume a timestamp from one delivery version points to the same words in every distribution copy.

Turn verified topic changes into chapter candidates

A chapter label should help the listener choose a topic. Replay the transition and give the section a descriptive title. An AI timestamped note is not automatically a chapter boundary, and a short technical explanation does not need an invented list of topics to look complete.

Frequently asked questions

Does a speaker label identify the person by name?

No. A label such as Speaker 1 organizes dialogue. Confirm a name from the recording and an authorized source before attributing a quote.

Are report timestamps ready for the final episode?

Treat them as approximate review locations. Check the original and map any excerpt offset to the final master. Recheck after cuts, speed changes or inserted audio.

Can I publish the report as VTT or SRT?

The current built-in exports are Copy, Word and PDF. A report is not a ready-to-publish VTT or SRT file; prepare and check any timed transcript separately.

Can I analyze a whole long episode for free?

One daily free analysis supports up to 10 minutes and 200 MB. Monthly Premium supports up to 45 minutes and 200 MB per upload. Use an authorized review excerpt when the recording exceeds your limit.

Can I paste a podcast platform URL?

The current workflow requires a video file upload. Export or obtain a permitted copy of your recording, then upload that file.
