How it works

01

Add your clip

MP4, MOV or WebM, 4 to 30 seconds. A file over 100 MB is shrunk on your device first in most current browsers.

02

Add reference photos

One to eight photos of the person, product or object to put in. You can also create a reference image from a description.

03

Generate and check

The result keeps the length and framing of your clip. Watch it through before you use it.

There are two kinds of swap. Swap a person or object replaces what is in the clip. Give my character this motion keeps the movement of the clip and applies it to your reference character.

What stays the same

  • The length of the clip
  • The framing and camera movement
  • The setting and background
  • The timing of the action

What to know first

  • Output is 480p, so it suits drafts and social tests more than final delivery
  • Voice references are not accepted in a swap
  • Our published example is a 4-second person swap; we have not yet published a product swap or a clip over 15 seconds
  • Generated video can contain glitches

Questions before you start

Can AI replace a person in an existing video?

Yes. You upload a clip of 4 to 30 seconds and one to eight reference photos. The AI studio generates a new version of the clip with the person replaced, keeping the length, framing and setting of the original.

Can I swap a product or an object, not only a person?

The swap accepts photos of people, products and objects. Our published example is a person swap; we have not yet published a product example, so test it on a short clip first.

How long can the clip be, and what does it cost?

Clips can be 4 to 30 seconds. Clips up to 15 seconds use 1 AI Credit per 10 seconds. Longer clips use 1 AI Credit per 4 seconds, so a 30-second swap uses 8 AI Credits.

What quality is the result?

Swaps are generated at 480p. That is enough for drafts, concept tests and many social placements, but it is lower than a 1080p master.

Do I need to analyze the video first?

No. The AI studio works on its own. If you do run an analysis first, the report can be used as a starting prompt.

Is it allowed to swap a real person into a video?

Only with that person's consent and the rights to the footage. Impersonation, misleading content and sexual content of real people are not allowed.
