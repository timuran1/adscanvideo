An analyzer should connect what is seen, said, and changed

A normal video summary often describes only the transcript. That misses camera choices, text overlays, demonstrations, reactions, edits, and silent actions. AdScanVideo combines selected visual frames with captions or a short-video transcript when one is available.

The result is designed to be used after the analysis: paste it into a brief, compare it with another creative, export it to Word or PDF, or turn the observations into a production plan.

Questions before you analyze

Can AI analyze an uploaded video?

Yes. Upload an MP4, MOV, WebM, or MKV file up to 200 MB and 10 minutes. The analyzer samples the timeline and returns a structured report.

Does the analysis include dialogue?

Dialogue is included when captions or a usable short-video audio transcript are available. Otherwise the report clearly states that it is visual-only.

Is there a free video analysis?

One analysis is free each day per network during early access. No account or credit card is required.

Are videos stored permanently?

No. The video and temporary audio, caption, and frame files are deleted when processing finishes, fails, or times out. We retain the analysis text and limited job and usage metadata so you can retrieve the result. Selected frames, available captions, and your request are sent to the configured AI provider during processing. Read the privacy details.
