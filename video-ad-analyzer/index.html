From reference ad to testing plan

This example shows the format and level of detail. Actual results depend on the video, selected mode, captions, audio quality, and visible evidence.

HOOK — Opens on the failed result before explaining the product.
AUDIENCE — Speaks to a buyer frustrated by setup time.
PROOF — Shows the product completing the task in one continuous demonstration.
PACING — Fast cuts in the first six seconds, then slows for proof.
CTA — Names the desired outcome but does not create urgency.

TEST NEXT — Keep the failure-first opening. Test a quantified outcome, a customer quote, and a stronger final offer.

What the report examines

  • First-frame clarity and opening hook
  • Target audience and implied pain point
  • Product demonstration and proof
  • Dialogue, overlays, captions, and claims
  • Offer structure and call to action

What it cannot tell you

  • Actual spend, conversions, or ROAS
  • Why an algorithm distributed the ad
  • Whether the claims are legally substantiated
  • Whether copying the creative is permitted
  • Which variation will definitely win
Use competitor ads for research and inspiration. Build original creative, verify claims, and follow the platform rules and intellectual-property rights that apply to your campaign.

Questions before you analyze

What is an AI video ad analyzer?

It reviews the creative itself: frames, available dialogue, overlays, sequence, hook, promise, proof, pacing, and CTA. It does not access private campaign performance data.

Can it analyze TikTok, YouTube, or Meta video ads?

You can upload a lawful copy of the file or try a public URL. Uploading is more reliable because platforms may block automated downloads.

Can I compare two ads?

Analyze each ad separately with the same mode, then compare the resulting hook, audience, proof, pacing, and CTA sections.

How should I use a competitor analysis?

Extract the strategic pattern, then create an original concept for your brand. Avoid copying protected footage, scripts, trademarks, or distinctive creative expression.
