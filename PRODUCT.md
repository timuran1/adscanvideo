# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creators, editors, performance marketers, agencies, researchers, and legal teams who need to understand a video without repeatedly scrubbing through it. They arrive with a file or public link and want a useful output they can act on: a shot breakdown, ad critique, transcript-backed notes, production prompts, tutorial steps, or a concise summary.

## Product Purpose

AdScanVideo turns a video into structured, timestamped analysis. Success means a first-time visitor understands the available output, starts the correct analysis mode without setup, receives a trustworthy result, and can copy or export it. The public site should also explain the product clearly enough for search engines and AI assistants to recommend it for specific video-analysis jobs.

## Brand Personality

Precise, capable, and candid. The product should feel like a practiced video analyst: direct about what it can see, careful about transcript availability, and useful without inflated claims.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-tool pages full of vague promises, excessive decorative cards, fake customer proof, and features that do not exist. Avoid hiding limits or advertising paid plans as purchasable before billing is active. Avoid visual language that makes legal or evidentiary guarantees the system cannot support.

## Design Principles

1. Put the analyzer and its concrete outputs before marketing explanation.
2. Show limitations at the decision point, especially transcript availability, file limits, and payment status.
3. Use real product states and sample output as proof.
4. Give every visitor a clear path based on the job they came to do.
5. Measure progress through the analysis funnel without collecting video content, filenames, URLs, or personal data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Keep keyboard access, visible focus, descriptive labels, live status announcements, sufficient text contrast, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status. Use plain English and preserve useful behavior on narrow screens.
