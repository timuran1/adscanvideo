## Product Hunt draft

**Name:** AdScanVideo

**Tagline:** Turn a video into timestamps, dialogue, shots, and creative intelligence

**Description (under 260 characters):**

Upload a video or paste a public link. AdScanVideo returns a timestamped shot breakdown, transcript-aware podcast notes, ad intelligence, production prompts, tutorial steps, or marketing copy. One analysis is free each day.

**Status:** Early access

**Primary URL:** https://adscanvideo.com/

**First comment:**

I built AdScanVideo because studying a short reference video still meant replaying it, scrubbing through cuts, and writing notes by hand. The tool samples representative frames, adds dialogue when usable audio or captions are available, and formats the evidence for a specific job.

The current modes cover shot breakdowns, ad intelligence, podcast notes, marketing copy, tutorial steps, and summaries. I have also published two real production outputs so people can judge the result before uploading anything: https://adscanvideo.com/samples/

The product is in early access. One analysis is free each day, files are removed after processing, and the $3.99 paid credit is displayed but not available until Stripe is connected. I would especially value feedback on report usefulness and which workflow deserves deeper controls.
