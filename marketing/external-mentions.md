### Indie Hackers: build lesson

**Working title:** I learned that a video transcript is not a video summary

I started building AdScanVideo as a way to turn short videos into structured notes. The first version made the common mistake of treating speech as the whole video.

That works for a static lecture. It fails when the evidence is a product demonstration, a before-and-after shot, a chart, a reaction, or text that appears without being spoken.

The workflow now combines a usable transcript with representative frames from across the timeline, removes near-duplicates, and keeps timestamps connected to each observation. If no transcript is available, the report says it is visual-only instead of inventing dialogue.

I wrote up the practical method and a reusable prompt here: https://adscanvideo.com/blog/how-to-summarize-a-video-with-ai/

I would value feedback from people working with video: which information do transcript-only summaries miss most often for you?
