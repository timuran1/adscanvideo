A shot list turns taste into instructions

A team cannot reliably reproduce “make it feel like this reference.” They need to know what the camera sees, what changes during the shot, how long the beat lasts, and what has to be prepared. A structured shot list makes those choices discussable before production.

AdScanVideo creates a first draft from sampled frames and available dialogue. A producer can then add the fields the source video cannot reveal, such as location, talent, props, equipment, crew, schedule, and budget.

Useful fields from the analysis

  • Shot number and timestamp
  • Framing and subject placement
  • Visible action and continuity
  • Camera movement and transition clues
  • Lighting, dialogue, and edit rhythm

Fields your producer should add

  • Location, talent, wardrobe, and props
  • Camera, lens, grip, and lighting equipment
  • Audio plan and approved script
  • Owner, priority, schedule, and status
  • Budget, rights, safety, and approvals
A sampled-frame analysis can infer visual changes, but it cannot know the original lens, lighting equipment, crew setup, or production intent. Treat technical specifics as a planning hypothesis.

Questions before you analyze

What is a video shot list?

It is a chronological production plan describing each shot, usually including framing, subject, action, camera movement, duration, audio, and production requirements.

Can AI make a shot list from an existing video?

Yes. AdScanVideo samples the timeline and produces a first draft with timestamps and visual details. A producer should add logistical fields and verify technical assumptions.

How long can the reference video be?

The current maximum is 10 minutes and 200 MB for uploaded files.

Can I export the shot list?

Completed reports can be copied or exported to Word and PDF.
