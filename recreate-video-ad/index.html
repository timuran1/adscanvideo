How it works

01

Write the prompt

Describe the shots in order: who, what they do, framing, light. Or run a Shot breakdown on a video and use the report as a first draft.

02

Add references

Name each photo and use the name in the prompt, for example @image1. Add a clip for the camera move, or a voice or sound.

03

Questions before you start

Can AI recreate a video ad?

It can generate a new video from your description of the ad. You write the shots, add reference photos of your own person or product, and get a new 1080p video of 5 to 30 seconds. It does not copy the original footage.

How do I get a prompt from an existing ad?

Upload the ad to the analyzer and choose Shot breakdown. The report lists each shot with framing, action and timing, and the AI studio can load it as the starting prompt for you to edit.

Can I use my own product and people?

Yes. Add up to 9 reference photos, give each a name and use the names in the prompt. You can also add a clip to follow for camera and motion, and up to 3 voice or sound references.

How long can the video be, and what does it cost?

5 to 30 seconds at 1080p. It uses 1 AI Credit per 15 seconds, so a 30-second video uses 2 AI Credits.

Is it legal to recreate a competitor's ad?

Borrowing a structure or an idea is different from copying. Do not reuse another brand's footage, music, trademarks, claims or people, and follow the rules of the platform where you publish.

Will the result match my reference exactly?

No. Generated video follows the prompt and references loosely and can contain mistakes. Check each result and expect to adjust the prompt.
