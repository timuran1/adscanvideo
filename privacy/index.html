Privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

AdScanVideo processes a video to create your report. We do not keep your uploaded video as a library.

Videos and analyses

We temporarily download or receive your video to extract frames, audio, and available captions. Selected frames, available captions or a short-video transcript, and your analysis request are sent through OpenRouter to the configured AI model. The video and temporary audio, caption, and frame files are removed when the analysis finishes, fails, or times out. The generated analysis text, source metadata, and limited operational records are stored on our server so the result can be retrieved; they are not automatically published. Do not submit content you do not have the right to process.

Browser access and free allowance

Your browser stores a random access token and the identifier of an active analysis so you can reconnect after reloading. Keep that token private. Clearing browser storage can remove access to previous analyses and any purchased or monthly allowance. You can save the private access key and restore it on another trusted browser; anyone who has that key can use the associated allowance. We use a keyed hash of your network address to enforce the daily free allowance. Shared networks share that allowance. Infrastructure logs may contain IP addresses.

Analytics and former waitlist

We use Google Analytics for traffic and product-usage measurement. Events may record the selected analysis mode, upload-or-link input type, a broad video-duration range, transcript availability, and whether an analysis started, completed, or failed. We do not send video filenames, video URLs, job identifiers, report text, contact details, or raw error messages to Google Analytics. Cloudflare provides website delivery and security. We no longer collect waitlist signups. Contact details submitted through the former waitlist were sent to Formspree; contact support if you want us to review a deletion request.

Payments

Stripe Managed Payments hosts checkout and processes the payment. You enter card details on Stripe's page, not in our analyzer. Stripe receives the checkout email and payment information needed to process your purchase. We retain a checkout identifier, purchase record, and credit balance to deliver the purchased analysis. For a monthly plan, we also retain Stripe subscription, customer, and paid-invoice identifiers, the billing-period dates, and analyses used against that period. We do not store full card details. See Stripe's privacy information on its checkout page for its handling of payment data.

Access, deletion, and contact

To ask about or request deletion of a report, email support@adscanvideo.com. Include the job identifier if you have it so we can locate the record, and use the payment email address for billing requests. Do not send passwords or full card details. We may need to retain limited transaction records required for accounting or disputes even after a report is deleted.
