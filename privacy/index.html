How your data is used

Updated September 20, 2026.

Videos and analyses

We temporarily download or receive your video to extract frames, audio and available captions. Selected frames, available captions or a short-video transcript, and your analysis request are sent through OpenRouter to the configured AI model. The video and temporary audio, caption and frame files are removed when the analysis finishes, fails or times out. The analysis text, source metadata and limited operational records are stored on our server so the result can be retrieved; they are not automatically published.

Browser access and daily allowance

Your browser stores a random access token and the identifier of an active analysis so you can reconnect after reloading. Keep that token private. Clearing browser storage can remove access to your analyses and future paid credits. We use a keyed hash of your network address to enforce the daily free allowance. Shared networks share that allowance. Infrastructure logs may also contain IP addresses.

Website analytics and waitlist services

The website uses Google Analytics for traffic and product-usage measurement. It records actions such as choosing an analysis mode, starting or completing an analysis, encountering a broad failure category, reaching the free allowance, and starting checkout. Analytics events may include the selected mode, upload-or-link input type, a broad video-duration range and whether a transcript was available. We do not send video filenames, video URLs, job identifiers, analysis text, contact details or raw error messages to Google Analytics. Cloudflare provides delivery and security. If you submit the waitlist form, your name and the contact details you provide are sent to Formspree so we can contact you about launch updates.

Payments

Payments are not available yet. When enabled, checkout will be hosted by Stripe. Card details will be entered on Stripe's checkout page, not collected by our analyzer.

Questions or deletion requests

Email hello@adscanvideo.com with questions or a request to delete your analysis. Include the analysis identifier if you have it; do not email passwords or payment card details.
