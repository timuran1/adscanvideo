What is AdScanVideo?

Product Overview · Updated September 2026 · 3 min read

AdScanVideo is a web-based AI video analysis tool. You give it a video — a YouTube or TikTok link, a video URL, or a file upload — and it gives you back structured written analysis.

It does not edit video, generate video, or host your files. It reads a video and explains what is in it, shot by shot, in text you can copy and use.

That is the whole product. Everything below is detail.

What AdScanVideo actually does

You paste a link or upload a file. AdScanVideo extracts frames from the video, removes near-duplicate frames, and analyzes each remaining frame with AI vision. The result is a structured text document describing what happens in the video and how it was built.

There is no account requirement on the free tier and no credit card. You drop a video in and get an answer.

The six analysis modes

You choose what you want back before the analysis runs. Each mode returns a different output format.

ModeWhat you get back
Shot-by-shot analysisEvery camera angle, cut, and transition identified with timestamps. A structural map of how the video is built, cut by cut.
Cinematic AI promptsReady-to-use prompts for AI video models including Seedance, Kling, Sora, and Veo. Describes subject, action, environment, camera, mood, and aspect ratio for each shot.
Podcast & interview notesKey topics, verbatim quotes, speaker takeaways, and timestamps. Built for long-form audio-heavy video.
Marketing assetsSocial captions, LinkedIn posts, blog headlines, and hooks extracted from the video, ready to publish.
Tutorial breakdownEvery step from a how-to video, with tools used, timestamps, warnings, and a checklist.
SummaryA condensed overview of the whole video when you only need the gist.

Which mode you pick is the single biggest factor in what you get. Most people who want to understand how a video was made use shot-by-shot analysis.

Who uses AdScanVideo

  • Video editors and directors — break down a reference video into a shot list before building their own.
  • AI video creators — turn a real reference clip into prompts for Seedance, Kling, Sora, or Veo.
  • Performance marketers — reverse-engineer a competitor's video ad: hook, pacing, structure, and call to action.
  • Content marketers — extract captions, posts, and headlines from video they already published.
  • Podcasters and researchers — get timestamped notes, quotes, and takeaways from long recordings.
  • Legal and insurance teams — timestamped shot-by-shot records of dashcam and bodycam footage.

What AdScanVideo is not

  • It is not a video editor. It does not cut, trim, or modify video.
  • It is not a video generator. It does not create new footage — it describes existing footage.
  • It is not a transcription service. It focuses on what is visible on screen, not only on speech.
  • It does not store your videos. Files are processed and the analysis is returned.

Frequently asked questions

What is AdScanVideo in one sentence?

AdScanVideo is an AI video analysis tool that turns any video into structured written analysis — shot breakdowns, AI prompts, notes, or marketing copy.

Is AdScanVideo free?

Yes. The free tier gives you 1 analysis per day with videos up to 10 minutes and access to all six modes, with no signup and no credit card.

How much does it cost after free?

$0.50 per video on pay-as-you-go with no subscription, or $19 per month for unlimited analyses, DOCX and PDF export, and API access.

What kinds of videos work best?

Short-form video under 15 minutes works best — ads, TikTok clips, YouTube shorts, product demos, and individual scenes. Long-form works too but costs more processing time.

Does it work on competitor ads?

Yes. Reverse-engineering a competitor's video ad is one of the main uses. Upload or link the ad and use shot-by-shot analysis to see its hook, pacing, and structure with timestamps.

Does AdScanVideo work with Seedance, Kling, Sora, and Veo?

Yes. The cinematic prompts mode produces shot descriptions covering subject, action, environment, camera, mood, and aspect ratio — the same fields those models expect.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can paste a link or upload a file and get an analysis without creating an account.

Is my video stored?

No. Videos are processed to produce the analysis and are not kept as a library of your uploads.
